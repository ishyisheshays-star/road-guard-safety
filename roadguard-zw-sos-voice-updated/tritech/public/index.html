How it works

From report to public map.

1

You report

Submit the incident with location, photos and description. You get a reference number.

2

Under review

Your report is saved as PENDING REVIEW and is not shown publicly.

3

Validator decides

A validator approves or declines it — and tells you why if it is declined.

4

Public map & alerts

Approved incidents appear on the map. Validators can also send live alerts.

RoadGuard SOS Assistant

A calm voice-guided assistant will collect the essential details and send a compact SOS request to the RoadGuard validator desk.

Important: RoadGuard SOS alerts the RoadGuard validator desk; it does not itself call or dispatch ambulance, police or fire services. If anyone is in immediate danger, call the locally verified emergency number now (Zimbabwe emergency numbers commonly include 112, 995 and 994; availability may vary).
